Nov 8 - 2026 2:27 - Zurich, CH. Two days ago, I went to the Women in Informatics meeting hosted by Winz club at UZH. I had gone to their hackathon the weekend before and my teammate invited me to their movie night. Here I had some really good pizza. Pizza in the US is more focused on amount of cheese on thicker bread. Pizza in Switzerland is thinner, which a sourdough base and contains toppings like eggplants. I find the eggplants redundant in flavor, but provides good nutrients. Pizza is one of the most useful symmetries in people's lives. If someday, you are given a oddly shapped pizza with jagged edges at random locations, it would ruin your experience more than you think.

Nov 10 - 2026 16:35 - Zurich, CH - ETH Hönggerberg. Today I had a mock exam for solid state physics, which I did poorly on. I wanted to take the exam home and not submit it at all, but the TA said that the exam will be helpful in testing the new AI exam grader that ETH is implimenting. On the exam, there were a question involving Bravais lattices. There are 14 Bravais lattices in 3D, each characterized by its own symmetry group. I have to say, my least favorite of them is the FCC lattice. It is a horrible lattice type. The one my exam was FCC and it was described horribly. It had horrible questions along with it. I like the BCC lattice much more. If the question had been about a BCC lattice, I would have thoroughly enjoyed any attempts answering them.
